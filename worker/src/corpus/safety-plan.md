# Safety plan

<!-- keywords: safety, crisis, krisis, danger, bahaya, suicide, bunuh diri, support, bantuan, emergency, darurat, alone, sendirian -->

## Immediate support

If you are in immediate danger, call your local emergency number now. In Indonesia, that is 112. The
app also bundles 119 ext. 8, a line staffed for people who need someone to talk to. Move away from
anything you could use to hurt yourself, and stay near another person if you can. This is not a test
of how strong you are. It is a way to put distance between a dangerous moment and the next decision.

## Trusted person

Choose one person you could contact tonight: a friend, a family member, a roommate, or a colleague.
You do not have to explain everything. A message as short as "I am not okay and I do not want to be
alone right now" is enough to start. If the first person does not answer, contact the next one. Being
specific about what you need, even if it is only company, makes it easier for them to respond.

## Crisis lines

A crisis line can stay with you while you decide what to do next. You can call without knowing what
to say; the person on the line is trained to help you find the words. If calling feels impossible,
ask someone nearby to call with you. Keep the number somewhere you can reach it without searching.
Reaching out early is allowed, and it does not mean you are a burden.
